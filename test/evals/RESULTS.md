### What the run establishes

**Recall is untouched by either trim.** Every arm named the wanted route on all
eleven named probes. Whatever the paragraphs do, they do not carry the routing.

### What it does not establish, and this is the larger half

**The trims route wider, and the sample cannot say it is real.** `current` names 21
routes, the trims 23 and 24, and both extra hits land on the two probes where
silence was the right answer. Against `current`, `no-among` is **3 probes wider and
1 tighter** — a two-sided sign test over four discordant pairs gives **p = 0.625**.
That is *no evidence*, not *no effect*: there is **one run per cell**, and
`agent-stack`'s `agent-evals/references/statistics.md` asks for three to five.

**A hypothesis was falsified here, which is why the third arm exists.** The trim was
expected to loosen routing by compressing *the boundary* — the half that says NOT
through this. `no-among` leaves every boundary intact and shows the same widening on
p01, p02 and p07. So the effect, if it is one, tracks the **comparative paragraph**,
not the boundary. The arm run to confirm a mechanism refuted it.

### The decision it authorised

**Not the trim.** The size argument that started this was worth about 14.5 KB across
the four operator files — less than the ~25 KB first estimated, because the router
texts are only part of the block. Directionally worse silence handling is not worth
that, and the evidence licenses the next experiment rather than the change.

**The next experiment is narrow**: p01, p02 and p07 only, three to five runs per
cell, `current` against `no-among`. Four discordant pairs cannot separate a real
effect from one terse sample; twelve to twenty can.
