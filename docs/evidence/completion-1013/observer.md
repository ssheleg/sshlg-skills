- B174 managed-block ownership and B180's relayed wording decision require their named owner/operator record before editing operator-authored text. They do not block local tests, research or already authorized releases.
- “All logs added” means useful diagnosable failures, release/install provenance and privacy-preserving receipts for the named workflows. It does not authorize copying private transcripts, full settings, credential values or arbitrary debug dumps into Git.
