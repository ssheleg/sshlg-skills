## Human prerequisites versus autonomous work

Genuine human boundaries are a required sign-in/consent or secret input, aesthetic labels from a human where the rubric demands them, a requested ownership change to operator-authored REPOSITORY-HANDOFF/star text, or spend beyond the explicit bound. They are conditional on the actual named task. Missing native-quality evidence, stale docs, source reconciliation and a safe feasibility packet are not automatically human gates.

The board tool/capability outage is an external prerequisite. Prepared asks and consumer fixtures can progress without it; delivery cannot. Provider/Figma/remote-backend proof remains unverified until the required authorized capability and test surface exist. This observer did not reprobe those services or borrow credentials, and does not claim they are absent everywhere.

No proof supports universal “no bugs” or “zero quality loss.” Current honest claims are narrower: measured source controls, preserved state, exact payload comparisons, dated host observations and exploratory task results. The final observer should require an explicit residual frontier, not an empty board manufactured from missing evidence.
