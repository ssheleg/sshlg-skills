The requested feature — three-tier certification

A verifier reads the diff it was handed. That is the definition of its context, and it means a class of defect is invisible to it by construction. A node is now closed by three independent readings, and the run may not advance until all three pass.

verifier-unit — the changed functions, classes and branches, plus the node's own check. Finds a branch nothing exercises, a boundary that moved.
verifier-seam — everything that can reach the change: callers, callees, implementors, shared state, the neighbours' tests. Finds a contract that moved under a dependent, the duplicate that did not get the fix.
verifier-product — documentation, scenarios, user-visible strings, the features sharing this path. Finds a documented behaviour that is now false, an interaction nobody listed.

Blind is the design, not a detail. Three readings that inform each other are one opinion with three signatures, so certify refuses a report whose prose cites another tier's verdict. A tier cannot pass on an empty scope — a report naming nothing it read is a rubber stamp, and three of those cost triple and read as triple the assurance. A tier cannot pass while carrying a breaks finding; two severities, no third.
